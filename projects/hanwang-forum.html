Friends from Malaysia and local collaborators in Lijiang came together around sustainable living, traditional values, Baba Nyonya food, music and art – creating exchange with local Dongba culture and Western musicians.

03

Carrying Dongba culture into a new context.

Penang Earth Fest 2025 · Malaysia

My main achievement was facilitating cultural exchange by bringing Lijiang's local Dongba culture into a Malaysian sustainability event. The cultural idea of people and nature as closely related created a meaningful bridge to Earth Fest's sustainability theme.

CULTURAL CURATION DONGBA CULTURE 4 CHINA-BASED VENDORS CROSS-BORDER COORDINATION PUBLIC ENGAGEMENT

Dongba cultural exhibition

Personally reached out to Dongba cultural practitioners and successfully invited traditional paintings of Dongba deities to be presented as an exhibition in Penang.

Culture with context

Connected the Naxi/Dongba relationship between people and nature with Earth Fest's sustainability theme, giving the cultural presentation a reason to belong beyond decoration.

China participation

Successfully invited four vendors from China to participate in Penang, expanding the event's representation of Chinese culture and creating direct cross-border engagement.

CURATION PROOF Dongba deity paintings presented as a cultural exhibition.
MOBILISATION PROOF Four China-based vendors brought into the Malaysia event.
CROSS-CULTURAL VALUE Lijiang heritage translated into a sustainability context for Malaysian audiences.

A cross-border cultural platform connecting Lijiang heritage, sustainability and Malaysian audiences.
